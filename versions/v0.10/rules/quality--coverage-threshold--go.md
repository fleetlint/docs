## Requirement

go-test-coverage runs with a threshold (`.testcoverage.yml` with `threshold.total`/`file`/`package`, or the action with a threshold input), or a stack-neutral floor applies (the `cover` recipe compares with a minimum, diff-cover `--fail-under`, codecov `target`).

## Why

A coverage number nobody enforces drifts down one "quick fix" at a time.

## Fix

Add go-test-coverage with `threshold: {total: 80}` in .testcoverage.yml, or compare the `go tool cover -func` total with COVER_MIN in the `cover` recipe.

Agent instruction: Add a threshold to the cover recipe using the stack profile's tool (go: go-test-coverage or a cover script with a minimum; python: --cov-fail-under; node: coverageThreshold; rust: cargo llvm-cov --fail-under-lines; kotlin: kover verify).

A finding of this rule points at `.testcoverage.yml`.
