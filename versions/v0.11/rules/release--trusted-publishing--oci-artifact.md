# release/trusted-publishing/oci-artifact

[release/trusted-publishing](release--trusted-publishing.md) does not apply to the `oci-artifact` artifact: nothing is checked for it.

## Why it does not apply

OCI registries have no trusted publishing; the forge's own registry takes the job token, and signing (release/signing/oci-artifact) is what identifies the publisher.
