# release/reproducible-build/oci-artifact

[release/reproducible-build](release--reproducible-build.md) does not apply to the `oci-artifact` artifact: nothing is checked for it.

## Why it does not apply

the artifact is whatever the step pushes (a bundle, a catalog, a module): its reproducibility is that of the files, which are checked where they are built. Not checked.
