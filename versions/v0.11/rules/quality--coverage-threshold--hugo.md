# quality/coverage-threshold/hugo

[quality/coverage-threshold](quality--coverage-threshold.md) does not apply to hugo: nothing is checked for it.

## Why it does not apply

A Hugo site has no tests to measure coverage of; scripts in the site are checked as their own stack.
