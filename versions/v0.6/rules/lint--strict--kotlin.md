## Requirement

The detekt configuration sets `active: true` for every rule in `params.required` (ruleset.Rule): swallowed and too-generic exceptions, empty catch blocks, method length and cognitive complexity. `detekt --generate-config` writes a full configuration to edit.

## Why

The compiler accepts a swallowed exception and a 400-line method; detekt is the only place these are refused, and only when the rules are on.
