## Requirement

maven-dependency-plugin's `analyze` runs with `failOnWarning` (bound in pom.xml or invoked as `dependency:analyze`), or the Gradle dependency-analysis plugin's `buildHealth` runs (in a recipe, a CI step, a task dependency, or with `severity("fail")`).

## Why

Every declared dependency is attack surface and update churn; one nothing uses is pure cost.

## Fix

Gradle: apply com.autonomousapps.dependency-analysis and run `buildHealth` in `audit`; Maven: bind dependency:analyze-only with failOnWarning.

Agent instruction: Add the stack's unused-dependency checker to the audit target; remove the dependencies it reports in a separate change after confirming nothing loads them dynamically.
