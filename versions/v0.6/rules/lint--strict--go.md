## Requirement

The golangci-lint v2 configuration enables every linter in `params.required` (errcheck, govet, staticcheck, errorlint, gosec, gocognit, funlen), through `enable`, `default: all` or the standard set, and does not list it under `disable`.

## Why

Swallowed errors, unsafe calls and oversized functions are exactly what these linters catch.
