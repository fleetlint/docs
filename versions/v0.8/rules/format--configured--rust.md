## Requirement

`cargo fmt` runs in a hook, the task runner or CI, or a `rustfmt.toml` is committed in the crate or a parent directory. Another formatter is added with `accept:`.

## Why

Without one formatter every editor reformats differently and diffs fill with whitespace churn that hides the real change.

## Fix

Add `cargo fmt --all --check` to the lint target and a cargo fmt hook, with a committed rustfmt.toml.

Agent instruction: Add `cargo fmt --all --check` to the lint target and a cargo-fmt pre-commit hook; reformat in a separate commit.

A finding of this rule points at `rustfmt.toml`.
