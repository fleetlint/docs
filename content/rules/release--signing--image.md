# release/signing/image

Release artifacts are signed

Implements [release/signing](release--signing.md) for the `image` artifact: it runs when the release publishes one (see [artifacts](capabilities.md#artifacts)).

## Requirement

The workflow that pushes the image signs it by digest (`cosign sign <image>@<digest>`, the digest from the build step's output), with GoReleaser `docker_signs:`, or with Notation. Signing by tag is a partial: a tag can be moved after signing.

## Why

Checksums prove integrity; only a signature proves who built it.

## Capability applies when

```cel
release.exists || repo.artifacts.size() > 0
```

## Fix

After the push step run `cosign sign --yes ${IMAGE}@${{ steps.build.outputs.digest }}` with `id-token: write` on the job (keyless) or `--key` on Gitea.

Agent instruction: Add sigstore/cosign-installer and, after the build-push step with an id, `cosign sign --yes <registry>/<image>@${{ steps.<id>.outputs.digest }}`; grant `id-token: write` and `packages: write` to that job only.
