## Requirement

A formatter is configured in the package or a parent directory: Prettier (`.prettierrc*`, `prettier.config.*` or a `prettier` key in package.json), Biome with its formatter enabled, or dprint; or one of them runs in a hook, the task runner or CI. Another formatter is added with `accept:`.

## Why

Without one formatter every editor reformats differently and diffs fill with whitespace churn that hides the real change.

## Fix

Add a Prettier configuration (.prettierrc.json) and the prettier hook, or use Biome.

Agent instruction: Add .prettierrc.json and prettier to devDependencies, run it from the fmt target and a hook; reformat in a separate commit.

A finding of this rule points at `.prettierrc.json`.
