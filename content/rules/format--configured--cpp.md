## Requirement

A `.clang-format` is committed in the project or a parent directory, or clang-format runs in a hook, the task runner or CI. Another formatter is added with `accept:`.

## Why

Without one formatter every editor reformats differently and diffs fill with whitespace churn that hides the real change.

## Fix

Add .clang-format (`fleetlint fix --apply` writes it) and the clang-format hook.
