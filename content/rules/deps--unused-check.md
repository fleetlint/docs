## Requirement

A task-runner recipe or a CI step runs the stack's unused-dependency check, so a dependency nothing imports is removed instead of carried (and audited, and updated) forever. Comments, `echo` lines and lines that only install the tool do not count.

## Why

Every declared dependency is attack surface and update churn; one nothing uses is pure cost.

## Fix

Add the stack's unused-dependency check to the `audit` target (see https://fleetlint.org/stacks/).

Agent instruction: Add the stack's unused-dependency checker to the audit target; remove the dependencies it reports in a separate change after confirming nothing loads them dynamically.

See: <https://fleetlint.org/baseline/#dependencies>
