## Requirement

golangci-lint has `dupl` enabled (in `enable`, or `default: all`) and not in `disable`; or a stack-neutral detector.

## Why

Near-duplicate implementations are how a fix lands in one copy and not the other; a detector makes the second copy visible at review time.

## Fix

Add `dupl` to `linters.enable` in .golangci.yml.

Agent instruction: Add the stack's duplication detector to the lint target with a threshold, and remove or merge the duplicates it reports rather than raising the threshold.

See: <https://fleetlint.org/baseline/#design>, <https://fleetlint.org/stacks/#go>
