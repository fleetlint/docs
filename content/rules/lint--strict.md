## Requirement

The linter configuration enables the checks the stack profile requires; each missing one is reported.

## Why

A linter with its defaults reports style; the checks that find swallowed errors, crashes and oversized functions are mostly off until they are switched on.

## Fix

Enable the reported checks in the linter configuration (see https://fleetlint.org/stacks/).

Agent instruction: Enable each reported check in the linter configuration and fix or ratchet the findings it produces instead of suppressing them.
