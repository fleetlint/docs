# release/trusted-publishing/helm-chart

[release/trusted-publishing](release--trusted-publishing.md) does not apply to the `helm-chart` artifact: nothing is checked for it.

## Why it does not apply

Chart registries have no trusted publishing; the forge's own registry takes the job token, and signing (release/signing/helm-chart) is what identifies the publisher.
