## Requirement

RuboCop is configured (`.rubocop.yml` in the project or a parent directory) or Standard is used (`.standard.yml` or the `standard` gem); a `.rubocop_todo.yml` is the ratchet, not a permanent allowlist.

## Why

Ruby's flexibility is only safe with a linter that holds the line on method length, complexity and swallowed exceptions.

## Fix

Add .rubocop.yml and the rubocop gems to the Gemfile's development group (`fleetlint fix --apply` does both).

Agent instruction: Add .rubocop.yml from the template, add rubocop with rubocop-performance and rubocop-rspec to the Gemfile, and run `rubocop --autocorrect`.

`fleetlint fix` can apply this rule's fix automatically (2 action(s)).
