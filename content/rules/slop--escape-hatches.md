## Requirement

Suppressions are rare and each has a reason next to it: `@ts-ignore`, `@ts-expect-error`, `as any`, `eslint-disable`, `nolint`, `NOLINT`, `noqa`, `type: ignore`, `pylint: disable`, `pyright: ignore`, `rubocop:disable`, `phpstan-ignore`, `@Suppress`, `@SuppressLint`, `[SuppressMessage]`, `#pragma warning disable`, Dart `ignore:` and Rust `#[allow]`. This rule lists them for review; it cannot tell a justified one from an unjustified one.

## Why

Each suppression switches a check off for good; a list of them is the fastest audit of where the checks do not apply.
