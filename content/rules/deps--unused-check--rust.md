## Requirement

A recipe or CI step runs cargo-machete, cargo-shear or cargo-udeps.

## Why

Every declared dependency is attack surface and update churn; one nothing uses is pure cost.

## Parameters

- `skip`: `^\s*[@+-]*\s*(\{\{[^}]*\}\}\s*|\$[({][A-Za-z_]+[)}]\s*)?(#|//|echo\b|printf\b|(sudo\s+)?(go (install|get)|cargo b?install|pipx? install|pip3 install|python3? -m pip install|uv (tool |pip )?(install|add)|(npm|pnpm|yarn|bun) (i|install|add|global add)|gem install|bundle add|brew install|dotnet tool (install|update)|composer (global )?require|apt(-get)? install|curl|wget)\b[^;&]*$)`

## Fix

Run `cargo machete` in the `audit` target.

Agent instruction: Add the stack's unused-dependency checker to the audit target; remove the dependencies it reports in a separate change after confirming nothing loads them dynamically.
