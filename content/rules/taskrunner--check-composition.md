# taskrunner/check-composition

check runs the gates and excludes bench and release

## Requirement

`check` runs lint and test, as prerequisites, through targets it depends on (up to three levels) or by invoking them in its recipe (`$(MAKE) lint test`, `just lint`), and never bench or release. Cover, audit and build belong in it too, but are not checked here.

## Why

`check` is the definition of green. A `check` that is slow or flaky gets bypassed, and a bypassed gate is worse than none; benchmarks are noisy and release is a deliberate act.

## Applies when

```cel
taskrunner.kind in ["make", "just", "task"] && "check" in taskrunner.targets
```

## Check

```cel
[("check" in taskrunner.deps ? taskrunner.deps["check"] : []) + ("check" in taskrunner.recipes
    ? taskrunner.targets.filter(t, taskrunner.recipes["check"].exists(l,
        l.matches("(\\$\\(MAKE\\)|\\$\\{MAKE\\}|\\bmake\\b|\\bjust\\b|\\btask\\b)[^;&|]*\\s" + t + "(\\s|$)")))
    : [])].exists(d1,
  [d1 + d1.map(d, d in taskrunner.deps ? taskrunner.deps[d] : []).flatten()].exists(d2,
    [d2 + d2.map(d, d in taskrunner.deps ? taskrunner.deps[d] : []).flatten()].exists(all,
      ["lint", "test"].all(t, t in all) && !("bench" in all) && !("release" in all))))
```

## Fix

Make `check` depend on lint, test, cover, audit and build, and move bench/release out.

Agent instruction: Rewrite the check target's prerequisites to `lint test cover audit build`; keep bench and release as separate targets.

A finding of this rule points at the file `taskrunner.file != "" ? taskrunner.file : "Makefile"` yields.
