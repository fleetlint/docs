## Requirement

The changelog is generated from the commit history, then edited: a stack-neutral generator (git-cliff in `cliff.toml`, `.cliff.toml`, `[tool.git-cliff]` or `[workspace.metadata.git-cliff]`; GoReleaser, whose changelog is on unless `changelog.disable`; release-please; cocogitto; semantic-release in `.releaserc*`, `release.config.*` or package.json `release`; commitizen) or the stack's own (changesets for npm, towncrier for Python), configured in its own file or in the manifest.

## Why

Hand-written changelogs drift from the commits; generated ones can be trusted and edited.
