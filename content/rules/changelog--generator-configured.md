## Requirement

The changelog is generated from the commit history, then edited: git-cliff (`cliff.toml`, `.cliff.toml`, `[tool.git-cliff]`, Cargo metadata), GoReleaser (its changelog is on unless `changelog.disable`), release-please, changesets, cocogitto, semantic-release or commitizen.

## Why

Hand-written changelogs drift from the commits; generated ones can be trusted and edited.
