## Requirement

A duplication detector runs in the task runner, the hooks or CI: golangci-lint `dupl` for Go, `jscpd` for any stack, PMD CPD for JVM, pylint `duplicate-code`, or Sonar. Another tool is added with `accept:`.

## Why

Near-duplicate implementations are how a fix lands in one copy and not the other; a detector makes the second copy visible at review time.

## Fix

Enable `dupl` in .golangci.yml, or add `jscpd` to the lint target (see https://fleetlint.org/stacks/ for the stack's tool).

Agent instruction: Add the stack's duplication detector to the lint target with a threshold, and remove or merge the duplicates it reports rather than raising the threshold.

See: <https://fleetlint.org/baseline/#design>
