## Requirement

git-cliff, commitizen or towncrier configured in pyproject.toml (`[tool.git-cliff]`, `[tool.commitizen]`, `[tool.towncrier]`) or towncrier.toml; or a stack-neutral generator.

## Why

Hand-written changelogs drift from the commits; generated ones can be trusted and edited.

## Fix
